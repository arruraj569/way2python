The idea

Combine arithmetic, comparisons, membership, and boolean operators to express expectations. and/or short-circuit, which helps guard an access that would otherwise fail. Parentheses make compound conditions easier to read.

INTERVIEW PRACTICE · EXPLAIN THE WHY

Talk it through.

Answer in your own words first. Then check the explanation and connect it to the programs above.

Do and and or always return booleans?

No. They short-circuit and return one of their operands. or returns the first truthy operand or the final operand; and returns the first falsy operand or the final operand.

Which common mistake can affect this program or test?

= assigns a value; == compares values. Do not use is to compare strings or numbers.
